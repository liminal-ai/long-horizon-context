## Binding Amendment D — APPROVED by Lee/Fable

Lee relayed the Fable phase-reviewer ruling on 2026-07-24 and independently
reproduced the behavior on this box's Node v24.18.0. The package engine floor
is Node `>=24.17.0`, and the TS lease/wake paths delegate their timestamp
strings directly to `Date.parse`.

Both private Rust parsers must follow the approved Node 24 contract:

- fixed numeric fields contain ASCII digits only;
- the two existing supported UTC shapes and their fixed separators remain
  strict;
- month is strictly `01..12`;
- day field is `01..31`; days beyond the month's natural length normalize
  through the calendar exactly like Node (non-leap February 29 → March 1,
  February 30 → March 2, February 31 → March 3, April 31 → May 1);
- hour is `00..23`, except exactly `24:00:00` / `24:00:00.000` normalizes to
  the next midnight;
- hour 24 with nonzero minute, second, or millisecond is invalid;
- day 00/32, month 00/13, hour 25, minute/second 60, signed fields,
  non-ASCII digits, and other previously approved malformed inputs remain
  invalid.

This supersedes the repair-r1 calendar-rejection ruling and its claims that
February 30/non-leap February 29/April 31 are invalid. Record Amendment D in
`PORT_STATUS.md`'s Phase-gate addendum, cite this Lee/Fable ruling, and correct
the r1/r2/r3 history so it does not continue asserting the superseded premise.
Do not silently reinterpret this as generic permissive parsing: only the exact
approved normalization above changes.
